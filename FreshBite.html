01 • Business Idea

Business Idea & Concept

FreshBite is designed as a technology-supported food delivery company that focuses on convenience, affordability, freshness and customer satisfaction.

Our Core Idea

The main idea of FreshBite is to prepare fresh meals and deliver them directly to customers. Customers can select meals according to their taste, budget and daily requirements.

Our goal is to make ordering quality food simple, fast and affordable.

Business Vision

FreshBite aims to become a trusted local food delivery brand known for reliable service, hygienic food preparation, reasonable pricing and a strong customer relationship.

Long-term vision: expand into multiple cities through a scalable delivery model.

02 • Business Location

Business Location

The proposed business should operate from an area with high population density and easy access to customers.

Recommended Area

  • Near universities and colleges
  • Close to office areas
  • Residential neighborhoods nearby
  • Easy road access for delivery riders
  • Availability of commercial kitchen space
  • Reasonable monthly rent

Why This Location?

  • Large number of potential customers
  • High demand during lunch hours
  • Strong evening food demand
  • Shorter delivery distances
  • Lower transportation cost
  • Opportunity for repeat customers
